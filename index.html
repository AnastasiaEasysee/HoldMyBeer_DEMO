Hold my Beer

Two minutes, one tray, and three rivals who want your beers.

How it plays

  • Load up at the bar. Walk up to the counter and the bartender fills your tray by themselves, one beer a second. Your tray holds 10. Leave whenever you like.
  • Serve a table. Walk up and tap Serve. The number above a table is points per beer. Far tables pay the most.
  • Keep your balance. Tilt your phone to lean and walk. The tray drags you off course, more with every beer and more the faster you go. Stopping hard makes the beers slosh.
  • Watch your step. Sticky spills slow you down, open cellar hatches swallow your whole tray, and customers walk around the room. Bumping into anything bounces you off hard. Bump a rival into a hatch and you score for every beer they lose.
  • Drink one. Away from the bar, tap Drink for a speed boost, a 7 s shield and an empty glass. You also see double for 5 s.
  • Throw the glass. Walk towards a rival so they're inside the fan in front of you (within 5 m) and tap Throw. You score points for every beer on their tray, and they drop 2.
